This presentation portal operates on a pure zero-tracking model. We do not use third-party tracking cookies, marketing pixels, social trackers (Meta, TikTok), or behavioral analytics services (Google Analytics, Hotjar). No non-essential cookies are stored on your device.

Our web servers automatically retain transient, strictly technical connection logs (timestamp, anonymized IP address, user-agent) purely to prevent denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and ensure system integrity. This processing is founded on our legitimate interest under GDPR Article 6(1)(f).

Security & Data Pipeline Audit

Certified Architecture Breakdown: Cloud Processing vs. Local BYOK Desktop Runtime

1. Code Audit Promise & Verified Local Sandboxing

The if. Desktop Application has undergone comprehensive independent static and dynamic security auditing. The client runs in an isolated local sandbox where 100% of screenplay text, scene logs, and character bibles remain exclusively on the filmmaker’s hardware. Zero telemetry or analytical tracking of script text is ever emitted to company servers.

2. Platform Data Processing & Cryptographic Retention

For projects hosted on the Web Cloud platform: all project metadata, timelines, and beat sheets are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest with AES-256. Creative assets are stored in isolated tenant database partitions. Backups are cryptographically rotated and purged upon account termination.

3. Strict Access Control & Zero Human Access Policy

We enforce an immutable Zero Human Access Policy: employees, contractors, and administrators cannot read, inspect, or export your creative writing. Automated database credentials are short-lived and tied directly to the author’s authenticated session.

4. Full Disclosure: Cloud API Processing & AI Model Training Notice

Full Radical Transparency: If you choose NOT to use the BYOK desktop client and instead use our standard Web Cloud AI features, your creative prompts and screenplay excerpts are transmitted to commercial cloud AI vendors (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) for inference.

Warning: Under standard consumer API terms, third-party cloud AI vendors may log inputs and retain data to train or fine-tune next-generation foundation models.

If your screenplay is subject to strict studio NDAs, WGA guild rules, or festival embargoes, we strongly urge you to download the if. Desktop Client with your own private BYOK endpoint, ensuring 100% intellectual property isolation.
